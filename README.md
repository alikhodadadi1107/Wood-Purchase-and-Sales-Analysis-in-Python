# Wood Purchase and Sales Analysis in Python  

## 📌 Project Overview  
This project focuses on analyzing **the company’s wood purchase and sales data** using Python.  
The workflow includes **data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and custom business insights** to help understand customer behavior, supplier performance, and product profitability.  

---

## 🛠️ Steps Performed  

### 1. Data Cleaning  
- Removed duplicate records  
- Standardized column values  
- Handled missing values  
- Prepared dataset for analysis  

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)  
- Summary statistics of sales and purchases  
- Trend analysis across months and years  
- Distribution of sales by customers, suppliers, and products  

### 3. Business Insights  
1. **Top 3 customers** with the highest purchases and their share of total company sales  
2. **Top 3 suppliers** with the highest sales and their share of total company purchases  
3. **Best customers per month** throughout the year  
4. **Best suppliers per month** throughout the year  
5. **Top 3 best-selling products** along with their net profit  
6. **Best-selling product per month** along with net profit  

---

## 📊 Tools & Technologies  
- **Python** (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)  
- **Jupyter Notebook** for analysis and visualization  

---

## 📈 Key Outcomes  
This project provided clear insights into customer and supplier performance, product profitability, and monthly sales dynamics, which can support **strategic decision-making** for the company.
